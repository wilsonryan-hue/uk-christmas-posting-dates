Free Christmas posting dates widget for your website

Every December, shop inboxes fill with "will it arrive by Christmas?". This widget answers it on your own site: a live countdown to Royal Mail's last posting dates, or to your own order-by dates if you need a day or two to pack. Paste two lines. No sign-up, no fee, no cookies.

Questions


How does the order-by date work? Set the packing days and the widget counts back that many working days (Monday to Friday) from each Royal Mail date. With 1 day, 2nd Class becomes "order by Thursday 17 December".


Does it slow my site down? No. It is one 6 KB file with no libraries, no fonts, no cookies and no calls to anyone else.


Can I remove the small credit line? Yes, add data-credit="off". It's MIT-licensed, so it's yours to use however you like.
